# Holes in book-making: where a story can drift or quietly end up wrong (2026-11-03)

Two read-only checks (story drift + process gaps), combined, duplicates merged.
Already being fixed by the running job: detailed looks, fighting, weapons, powers, skills, place interiors, item powers and spell details (decided just in time, then locked).

## 🔴 Likely to show up in a real book
H1. **A chapter never sees how the last one ended, or what really happened earlier.**
  - The writer gets the plan, not the actual text. Chapter 4 ends with her bleeding in a cellar at midnight, and chapter 5 opens at breakfast in the village. Scenes repeat, and characters get introduced twice.
  - The tracker only follows 16 names and 8 people (cut to 3 when the instructions get too long), so in a big cast, character #20's lost hand comes back.
  - **Fix:**
    - give the writer and the reviewer the end of the previous chapter plus a running "what actually happened" summary;
    - choose who to track by who is in the chapter, not by a fixed number.

H2. **Injuries, belongings, who's alive and where everyone is aren't tracked.**
  - Limping in chapter 3, running in chapter 4. He loses the knife in chapter 6 and draws it in chapter 8.
  - Magic costs (a nosebleed, exhaustion) vanish by the next chapter.
  - **Fix:** track injured, carrying, lost, alive or dead, and where now, per character per chapter. The book writer already has this list for sequels. Check every chapter against it.

H3. **Your "content limits" never reach the writer** ("no graphic violence"). Themes and the central question don't either.
  - **Fix:** put them in every chapter's instructions and in the editor's checks.

H4. **New minor names drift:** "Bram" becomes "Brannock", "Saltmere" becomes "Salt Mere".
  - **Fix:** collect every new name after each chapter into a name list, pass it to later chapters, and run the spelling check automatically.

H5. **A hand edit to a chapter never reaches the later chapters.** You remove her broken arm in chapter 5, and chapter 6 is still told it's broken.
  - **Fix:** when a chapter changes, track it again and flag the later chapters for a re-check.

H6. **The writer AI can approve its own chapter** when only one AI is working, with a bare "APPROVE" and no reasons.
  - **Fix:** approve automatically only with a different AI reviewing, at least one reason, and real confidence. Otherwise pause and ask you.

H7. **A sequel starts from book 1's beginning, while saying it starts from the end.**
  - "What changed" only runs for books already in a series.
  - Locked facts, items and status (dead or injured), Character Forge details and book 1's plot don't carry over.
  - **Fix:** run "Find what changed" automatically before a sequel, and carry the locked facts, items, status, details and a book 1 summary.

## 🟠 Possible
H8. **Days, seasons and travel times.** "Three days' ride", then arriving "the next morning". Snow, then blossom a week later.
  - **Fix:** track story day, season and date, plus a simple travel-time table.

H9. **Who knows which secret, and relationships changing.** She reveals the betrayal in chapter 5, and he's surprised by it again in chapter 9.
  - **Fix:** a "who knows what" list and a relationship line for each pair of characters.

H10. **Viewpoint and tense are never decided** unless "my style" is on. Chapter 6 can come out in first-person present while the rest is third-person past.
  - **Fix:** decide them when the book is invented and check every chapter.

H11. **Setups that never pay off, subplots dropped, side characters vanishing.** The locked box is never opened; a friend disappears after chapter 4.
  - **Fix:** after the last chapter, check each setup and each named character against the page.

H12. **The main character's change and the ending vs. the premise:** only scored in the final report, never fixed.
  - **Fix:** a last-chapter check that can send it back for a fix.

H13. **Instructions to the writer are quietly cut** when they're too long: facts, voice cards, must-shows (max 5), story rules (the newest dropped first).
  - **Fix:** cut the least important first, and show a visible ⚠️ listing what was cut.

H14. **"Approve anyway" and auto-approve skip the editor and the plan check for good,** and the report doesn't say so.
  - **Fix:** run them as notes anyway, and list "approved without checks" in the report.

H15. **Pictures and face locks are never remade when a description changes.**
  - **Fix:** remember which description each picture was made from, and mark it out of date when that changes.

H16. **Saving keeps old files when a part fails** (old Book Bible, cover, pictures), with only a log line.
  - **Fix:** say "not updated" and rename the old files "(older version)".

H17. **The whole-book report only reads about 2% of a long book.**
  - **Fix:** show a "sampled" banner and offer a full read in parts.

H18. **Accented letters (ọ, ẹ, ł, Greek):**
  - the PDF can fail at the very end;
  - captions, map labels and the cover lose accents or show "?";
  - the language is always set to English.
  - **Fix:** check letters when a chapter is approved, use a font that has every letter, and use the book's language.

H19. **Money, prices and distances** have no record.
  - **Fix:** a small ledger in the tracked state.

## 🟡 Minor
H20. **The editor's rewrites can't see the tracked continuity,** so a rewrite can undo it.
H21. **When the AI is busy, a weaker continuity reading becomes the record** with no warning.
H22. **Chapter pictures follow the plan, not the written text.**
H23. **The blurb, cover, Book Bible and book are never compared;** the cover also keeps its old title after the book is renamed.
H24. **The trailer isn't marked out of date** when the book or the looks change.
H25. **Alfred may act on another book's run** when two runs exist.
H26. **Cut-off AI replies waste retries** (they're caught, but slowly).

## Already well covered (don't redo)
- ages and years;
- weather, light and time of day;
- story rules and protected mysteries;
- the main character's name;
- voice cards and repeated phrases;
- word counts;
- planned beats, scene cards and must-shows (mostly);
- your own written text is never overwritten;
- the book is only put together when every chapter is approved, and redone when any chapter changes;
- duplicate titles;
- explicit confirmation for "Approve anyway".
