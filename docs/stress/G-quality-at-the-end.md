**1. G-18-a ⚠️ — the AI times out (once; and every time)**

- **What happens:** One timeout of the reviewer AI loses the whole-book review (and with it the opening and improve passes); the step blames “no independent AI was working”. - **How to reproduce:** `cd akowe && python -m tools.stress.run --section G --parts 18 --stress a` (ID G-18-a)
- **Likely cause:** The whole-book reviewer's job is made with `retry_busy=False`, so a timeout / busy / usage-limit answer is not waited for or retried: the reviewer row is `failed` at once (only AI_DECLINED gets a second ask and a stand-in). - **Suggested fix:** Let the run's whole-book review use the normal busy / usage-limit wait (retry_busy=True, as the beta reader does) or retry a failed reviewer once; when reviewers failed, say “The reviewer AI could not answer (TIMEOUT) — 📨 Ask the other AIs on the report page” instead of “no independent AI was working”.
