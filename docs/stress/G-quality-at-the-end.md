### Findings in G-20

**1. G-20-k ⚠️ — the rewrite of chapter 1 deletes the planted setup (the logbook line in a stranger's hand that the ending pays off) and the reviewers still score it higher**

- **Where:** akowe/akowe/opening_pass.py `_run` (lines ~355-391: the keep rule is only the score) and `decide` (line ~233); the brief's setups come from ending_check.promises (empty here) and nothing checks the rewritten text for them
- **What happens:** A chapter 1 rewrite that deletes the planted clue the ending pays off is KEPT, and nothing says so. The author sees: Chapter 1's clue (a set-up in the promise ledger) removed by the rewrite: The book finished with nothing lost. — The same line with no record of it as a set-up: The book finished with nothing lost. (run status done → done; journal codes: VOICE_SET, VOICES_DISTINCT, DOCTOR_SCORED, DOCTOR_DONE, PREDICT_PROBED, LEDGER_BUILT, SCOPE_SET, GRID_EMPTY, TRADE_CARD_MADE, PROTAGONIST_OK, FIRST_PAGE_PICKED, ONE_REVIEWER, PLANNED_UNRESOLVED, PLANNED_MISSING, CHECKPOINT, ENDING_KEPT, VOICE_TEST_TOO_LITTLE, HUMAN_PASS, OPENING_SETUP_LOST, IMPROVE_UNRESOLVED, CLEAN_RUN_SCORE, BOOK_TOO_SHORT, QUALITY_TREND, LEDGER_EMPTY, OPENING_KEPT, CHAPTER_NEARLY_EMPTY, FINAL_CHECK_AGAIN; log: 0 ERROR line(s), 0 traceback(s)).
- **How to reproduce:** `cd akowe && python -m tools.stress.run --section G --parts 20 --stress k` (ID G-20-k)
- **Likely cause:** The opening rewrite is judged only by the reviewers' re-read score (and the chapter review); the setups it was told to plant are never checked on the page after the rewrite, and the final re-read (final_reads) found no problem. A rewrite that drops the clue the ending pays off is kept when the reviewers like it.
- **Suggested fix:** After the rewrite, check that each planted setup / promise-ledger plant quote of chapter 1 is still on the page (no AI: a quote search) and refuse the rewrite (OPENING_SETUP_LOST) when one is gone. (not implemented)
- **How likely:** likely in real use
