Pair your phone
  1. Make sure the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. Open the phone's camera and point it at the code. Tap the link that pops up.
  3. Add it to your home screen: on iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android, ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

If the phone can't open it: the first time, Windows may ask whether AutoInjector may use the network — choose Allow (private networks).

🌍 From anywhere (Tailscale)

Away from home, your phone reaches this PC through Tailscale — a free app that links your own devices privately (nothing is opened to the internet).

  1. On this PC: install Tailscale from tailscale.com/download and sign in.
  2. On your phone: install the Tailscale app (App Store / Play Store) and sign in with the same account.
  3. Keep Tailscale switched on on both. This section then shows a second code.

  1. On the phone, turn Tailscale on.
  2. Scan this code (once is enough — it works at home and away).
