## Why this concept for a validation run

Chosen to exercise the machinery, not to manufacture failures:

- **Character continuity** — Marla carries objects, sustains an injury, and her knowledge changes at fixed chapters
- **World state** — fields, fences and the line itself *move* between chapters; locations must persist and change
- **Setups / payoffs** — the engraved lighter, the 1994 field book, the moved stake at Cobb's fence
- **Timeline dependencies** — the survey runs on tides and dates; travel between headlands is measurable and falsifiable
- **Knowledge matrix** — Marla, the town, and the reader each learn different things at different times
- **Revisions** — the midpoint reveal forces a documented retcon of an earlier chapter
