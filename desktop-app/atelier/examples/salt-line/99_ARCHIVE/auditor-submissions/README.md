# AUDITOR SUBMISSIONS — quarantine

Prose or manuscript-shaped material originating from the Auditor is placed here
on receipt and is **never merged** (§3.3: the Auditor writes nothing into the
manuscript or the bibles, ever).

Material here may be **cited** in a finding — "the Author's CH01 does X,
consider Y" — but has no position on the Established Reality ladder (§23) and
cannot become manuscript without a Showrunner ruling that would first have to
overturn §3.3.

**Currently empty.** No Auditor-authored artifact has ever been received by this
pipeline. Nothing has been preserved, deleted, validated, or rejected here,
because nothing has been delivered. This directory is the destination **if such
an artifact is ever actually received.**
