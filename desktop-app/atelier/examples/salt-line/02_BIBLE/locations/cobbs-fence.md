- geography: a shallow bowl running down to the shingle; the seaward third is
  visibly dead — grey stalks, white crust in the furrows
- layout: post-and-wire fence along the seaward edge, replaced in sections;
  **stake 11 of the survey line stands 40 m seaward of the 1994 position**
