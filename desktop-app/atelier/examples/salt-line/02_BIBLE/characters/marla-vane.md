# MARLA VANE

## STATIC
- full_name: Marla Jean Vane · age 34 · b. 1992
- physical: 5'7", narrow, weathered younger than her age; dark hair cut short
  enough not to need thinking about; grey eyes; a white scar across the left
  thumb knuckle from a surveying chain, age nineteen
- clothing: her father's oilskin, too big in the shoulders; boots resoled twice
- voice: level, unhurried. Answers questions slightly after people expect
- speech_patterns:
  - verbal_tics: says "roughly" when she means precisely, and vice versa
  - vocabulary_register: technical when uncertain, plain when sure
  - **what_they_never_say:** never says "I think" about a measurement, and never
    uses a person's first name when she is angry with them
- personality: methodical, literal, privately funny
- strengths: patient, exact, physically tireless
- weaknesses: mistakes accuracy for honesty; cannot let a wrong number stand
- skills: surveying, tidal arithmetic, dinghy handling, her father's shorthand
- beliefs: a measurement is a promise
- desire (want): to do the survey correctly and be believed
- need: to stop arbitrating her father's character and choose for herself
- fears: that she is only competent, not good
- secrets: she did not visit him in the last month of his illness
- backstory: left at 22 for inland contract work; came back at 33 when he got ill
- internal_conflict: mercy vs. accuracy, in her own hands for the first time
- arc: dutiful → complicit-by-inheritance → deciding → accountable
- signature_possessions: her father's brass lighter engraved **V.R.** · his 1994
  field book, unopened until Ch6 · a survey chain older than she is
