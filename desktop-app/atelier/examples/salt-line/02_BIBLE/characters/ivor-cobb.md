# IVOR COBB

## STATIC
- full_name: Ivor Cobb · age 61
- physical: heavy through the shoulders, hands cracked at the knuckles; walks
  with a list to the right from a hip that set badly
- voice: slow, fond, and completely unsentimental about land
- speech_patterns: names fields rather than owners — "the long acre", "the wet
  corner". **Never says "my land."**
- personality: generous, stubborn, entirely without self-pity
- strengths: knows the ground better than any instrument
- weaknesses: has known the line was wrong for years and said nothing
- desire (want): one more season in the long acre
- need: to stop being the reason a lie continues
- secrets: he asked Marla's father for the first favour, in 1994
- arc: neighbourly → betrayed → the one who tells her to publish

## DYNAMIC — as of CH12 (4 June)
- current_location: Cobb farm · condition: sound; hip lists (set badly 1998)
